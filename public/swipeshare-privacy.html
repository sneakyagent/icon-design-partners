SwipeShare — Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 16, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how the SwipeShare mobile application (“SwipeShare,” “the app,” “we,” “us”), published by Icon Design Partners, handles information. SwipeShare helps you pick your favorite photos and turn them into a collage to share. We designed it so your photos stay on your own device.

Your photos

SwipeShare asks for permission to access your photo library so you can browse photos by date range or album, swipe to choose your favorites, and build a collage. Your photos are processed only on your device. We do not upload, copy, or store your photos on any server, we cannot see them, and they are never used for advertising.

If you choose to save a collage, the app asks for permission to add it to your photo library. You can limit or change photo access any time in Settings → SwipeShare → Photos (iOS) or in your device’s app permission settings (Android).

Sharing

When you tap Share, your collage is handed to your device’s share sheet and sent only to the app you pick (for example, Instagram or Facebook). What happens after that is governed by that app’s own privacy policy. SwipeShare does not post anything on your behalf and does not connect to your social media accounts.

No accounts

SwipeShare does not require you to sign up or log in, and we do not collect your name, email address, or contacts.

Advertising

SwipeShare displays ads through Google AdMob. To serve and measure ads, Google’s advertising SDK may collect and process certain information, such as a device advertising identifier (IDFA on iOS, Advertising ID on Android), approximate location derived from IP address, device type and settings, and ad-interaction data. This is used to deliver ads, limit repetition, and measure performance. It does not include your photos. You can learn how Google uses this information at policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.

Tracking & your choices

On iOS, the app asks for your permission before using your advertising identifier for personalized ads (Apple’s App Tracking Transparency prompt). If you decline, you will still see ads, but they may be less relevant. You can change this any time in Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking on your device.

If you are in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom, Switzerland, or another region where consent is required, the app shows Google’s consent form before any ads load so you can choose how your data is used for advertising. On Android, you can reset or delete your Advertising ID in your device’s Google settings.

Analytics

We do not use third-party analytics SDKs to profile you. Basic, aggregated crash and delivery information may be provided by the app store platform (Apple or Google) in a form that does not identify you.

Children

SwipeShare is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

Data retention & deletion

Because your photos and collages stay on your device, you control them. Collages you save are stored in your photo library, where you can delete them like any other photo. Uninstalling the app removes its settings. For advertising data handled by Google, see Google’s policy linked above.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Material changes will be reflected by updating the “Last updated” date above.
